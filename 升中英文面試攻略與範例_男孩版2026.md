# 升中英文面試攻略與回答範例（男孩版）

> 對象：小六男生，班內前三、全級約十名，鋼琴六級，數理及記憶力較好，喜歡平板與科技。
> 用法：先看「一、面試流程」，再用「三、自我介紹」及「四、常見問題」逐題練；答案為範本，**必須改成自己的真事**，不要逐字背。

---

## 一、band 1 中學英文面試通常考什麼

| 環節 | 時間 | 考什麼 | 男孩常見失分 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 自我介紹 | 30–90 秒 | 表達、自信、英語流暢度 | 背得太熟、像錄音機；只講成績 |
| 個人問題 | 3–6 題 | 性格、興趣、價值觀、解難 | 一句起兩句止；答案沒有例子 |
| 朗讀文章 | 1 分鐘 | 發音、語調、斷句 | 讀得太快、不看標點、不懂的字停頓 |
| 看圖描述 | 1–2 分鐘 | 觀察、組織、時態 | 只說 "I see a boy"，沒有細節與推測 |
| 小組討論 | 5–10 分鐘 | 合作、禮貌、英語互動 | 搶話、只對老師講、不回應別人 |
| 數理／邏輯題 | 1–2 題 | 思路、表達步驟 | 只說答案，不說過程 |
| 反問學校 | 1 題 | 誠意、準備程度 | 「沒有問題」 |

> 面試官最想看三件事：**聽得明、講得到、教得入**。英語有小錯不要緊，停頓 too long、低頭、細聲才是最傷。

## 三、自我介紹（三段式）

### 結構

1. **基本資料**：姓名、學校、年級。
2. **兩個亮點＋一個例子**：成績／數理／鋼琴／好奇心，各用一句證據。
3. **為何想入這間學校＋結尾**。

### 範本 A：30 秒版（適用於計時／排隊時）

> Good morning, teachers. My name is **Chan Tai Man**. I am in Primary 6 at **ABC Primary School**.
> I am a curious boy who likes Maths and Science. I am in the top three of my class, and I have just passed ABRSM Grade 6 piano.
> In my free time, I like exploring new technology. I hope I can study here and learn more. Thank you.

### 範本 B：60–90 秒版（主版本，建議背到「滾瓜爛熟但自然」）

> Good morning, teachers. My name is **Chan Tai Man**. I am in Primary 6 at **ABC Primary School**.
>
> I would describe myself as **curious and hard-working**. I am in the top three of my class and around the top ten in my form. My favourite subjects are Maths and Science, because I enjoy finding out **why** things work. When I meet a difficult problem, I like to try different ways until I solve it.
>
> I have been learning the piano since I was six. I passed **ABRSM Grade 6** last year. Piano taught me that practice is boring sometimes, but small steps every day really work.
>
> In my free time, I like **watching videos about robots and trying simple coding** on my tablet. I am also interested in how AI can help people.
>
> I hope I can join your school, use your STEM facilities, and grow with your teachers. Thank you.

### 範本 C：加入「學校特色」的版本（自行階段最重要）

> ...（同上）... I know your school has a strong **STEM / robotics team** and a **music programme**. I would love to join the robotics club and also play piano in the school orchestra. I believe I can contribute and also learn from your teachers.

> 注意：學校名字、課程、校隊要事前查清楚，講錯比不講更差。

### 自我介紹的「三不」

- 不逐項報告成績（「我中文 95、英文 93、數學 100」）— 講排名就夠。
- 不背到零停頓、零表情 — 每段之間停半秒，望一望老師。
- 不講「我冇缺點」— 太假。

## 四、常見問題＋回答範例

### Q1. Tell me about yourself.

用上面範本 B。若老師說 "In one sentence"，就用：

> I am a curious P.6 boy who loves Maths, Science and piano, and I enjoy exploring how technology works.

---

### Q2. Why do you want to study at our school?

**結構**：學校特色（1）＋我能貢獻（1）＋我想學（1）。

> I want to study here for three reasons. First, your school has a strong **STEM programme and robotics team**, and I am really interested in technology. Second, I play the piano and I would like to join the **music activities** here. Third, I hope to become a more independent learner under your teachers. I am hard-working and curious, and I believe I can contribute to the school.

**追問：Why not another school?**

> I have looked at a few schools, but this one matches my interests best. I like the balance between **academic work and hands-on activities** here.

---

### Q3. What is your favourite subject? Why?

> My favourite subject is **Mathematics**. I like it because there is usually one clear answer, but many ways to reach it. For example, when I learned fractions, I tried drawing and also writing equations. When I finally got the right answer, I felt very satisfied. **Science** is my second favourite, because we can do experiments and see the result immediately.

**追問：What topic do you like most?**

> I like **space and energy**. I once watched a video about how solar panels work, and I tried to explain it to my classmates.

---

### Q4. What are your strengths?

選兩個，每個都要有例子（STAR：Situation–Task–Action–Result）。

> I think I have two strengths. The first is **curiosity**. When I see something new, I want to know how it works. Once I saw a self-checkout machine and I spent the weekend watching videos to understand the scanner. The second is **memory**. I can remember formulas and English vocabulary quite quickly, so I can spend more time on understanding. I am still learning to use this strength wisely.

---

### Q5. What is your weakness?

**原則**：講一個真但可改善的缺點＋具體行動，不要講「我太追求完美」這類假缺點。

> My weakness is **time management, especially with screens**. Sometimes I watch one more technology video and forget the time. Now I set a **25-minute timer**, finish my homework first, and use the tablet only after that. I also put it in another room when I study. I am not perfect yet, but I am improving.

> 若老師追問「咁你係咪機不離手？」：
> No, not anymore. I still enjoy technology, but I have a rule: **homework and piano first, screen time later**. My parents also help me check.

---

### Q6. What do you do in your free time? / Do you play games on your tablet?

**策略**：承認＋分類（遊戲 vs 學習）＋展示自控。不要只答 "I play games."

> I use my tablet mainly for two things. One is **learning**: I watch videos about robots, simple coding and science experiments. The other is **relaxing**: I sometimes play a short game with my classmates, maybe 20–30 minutes at the weekend. I enjoy technology because it makes me curious, but I know I need balance, so I always finish my work first.

**加分句**：

> My favourite channel is about **how things are made**. I like seeing the machines inside a factory.

---

### Q7. Tell me about your piano learning.

> I started learning the piano when I was six. I passed **ABRSM Grade 6** last year. I practise about **45 minutes a day** on school days and more at the weekend. My favourite piece is **「給愛麗絲」/ Für Elise**, because the melody is beautiful and it sounds harder than it is. Piano taught me **patience**: if I practise a difficult bar slowly every day, it becomes easy. I also performed in the school concert last year, and I was nervous but proud.

**追問：Will you continue in secondary school?**

> Yes. I hope to join the school orchestra or music team, and I would like to try Grade 7 when I have time.

---

### Q8. What kind of books do you read?

> I read both **non-fiction and stories**. I like books about **science and inventions**, because I can learn something new on every page. I also enjoy adventure stories. Reading helps my English, especially vocabulary and sentence patterns.

**追問：Tell me about a book you like.**

> I like a book about **great inventors**. I learned that many inventors failed many times before they succeeded. It taught me not to give up after one mistake.

> 不要假裝讀過很深的書；講一本你真的讀過的，加一個得著就夠。

---

### Q9. Describe a problem you solved. / Tell me about a challenge.

> Last year, our group had to build a small **water filter** for a Science project. At first, the water was still dirty. We tested three times and changed the order of the layers. Finally, the water became clearer. I learned that **testing and changing one thing at a time** is better than guessing. I also learned to listen to my groupmates.

---

### Q10. Are you good at working in a group?

> Yes, I enjoy group work. I usually **listen first**, then suggest one idea. If we disagree, I ask "What if we try both?" I am not the loudest person, but I try to keep the group on task.

**追問：What if a group member does nothing?**

> I would ask him what part he wants to do, and give him a small job first. If he still does not help, I would tell the teacher politely.

---

### Q11. What do you want to be when you grow up?

> I am not 100% sure yet, but I am interested in **engineering or AI research**. I like solving problems and building things. I also know that technology should help people, so I want to learn how to use it well. If I change my mind later, I hope the school can help me explore different areas.

> 避免只答 "I want to be rich / a boss."

---

### Q12. Who do you admire? / Who is your role model?

> I admire my **father**. He is an engineer and he always explains things patiently. When I ask him why the lift works, he draws a simple picture for me. He taught me that **asking questions is not stupid**.

> 也可以講老師、運動員、科學家；重點是「他教會我什麼」。

---

### Q13. Which is more important: marks or learning?

> I think **learning is more important**, but marks are useful feedback. If I only chase marks, I may forget what I learned after the exam. If I really understand, the marks usually follow. Of course, I still try my best in every test.

## 五、看圖描述（Picture Description）

### 四步法

1. **一句總結**：This picture shows ...
2. **前景／背景**：In the foreground / In the background ...
3. **細節＋推測**：He seems to be ... because ...
4. **感受／連結自己**：It reminds me of ...

### 範例（圖：一個男孩在課室用平板，旁邊有同學）

> This picture shows a classroom. In the foreground, a boy is using a tablet. He looks focused and curious. Maybe he is watching a science video or checking a robot design. In the background, two classmates are discussing a poster. I think they are doing a group project. The classroom looks bright and organised. This reminds me of my Science lessons, when we use tablets to look up information and then share our ideas. I think technology is useful, but we should use it for learning, not only for games.

**常用句**：

- In the foreground / background, ...
- He seems to be ... / It looks as if ...
- Maybe / Perhaps ...
- On the left / right, ...
- This reminds me of ...

## 六、小組討論（Group Discussion）

### 題目例子：Should students be allowed to use tablets in school?

**開場（不要霸住講）**：

> Shall I start? I think tablets can help us learn, but we need rules. What do you think?

**表達立場**：

> In my opinion, ... / I agree with you, but ... / That's a good point. I also think ...

**回應別人（必做）**：

> I agree with **what he said about** ..., and I want to add that ...

**收尾**：

> So, we agree that tablets are useful **if** we use them for learning and limit game time. Thank you.

### 加分／扣分

| 加分 | 扣分 |
|---|---|
| 望住組員、點頭 | 只望老師 |
| 叫得出組員名字（"I agree with Jason"） | 打斷別人 |
| 讓別人講完 | 一個人講足全場 |
| 用 "because" 講理由 | 只講 "Yes / No" |

## 七、數理／邏輯題示範

老師通常不是要你心算最快，而是看**你怎樣講思路**。

### 例 1：24 遊戲（用 3、3、8、8 砌 24）

> May I think for a moment? ... I would try 8 ÷ (3 − 8/3). Hmm, let me check: 3 − 8/3 = 1/3, and 8 ÷ 1/3 = 24. Yes, that works.

### 例 2：Pattern

> 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ___
> The differences are 3, 5, 7, 9, so the next difference is 11. The answer is 37.

### 例 3：應用題（速度）

> A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. What is its speed?
> Speed = distance ÷ time = 120 ÷ 2 = **60 km/h**. May I explain my steps? I first write down the formula, then substitute the numbers.

### 若不懂

> I am not sure, but I would try **this** way first... Could I have one hint?

> 誠實＋肯試，比亂講答案好。

## 八、朗讀（Reading Aloud）技巧

1. 先望一次全段，找不懂的字，**不要停**。
2. 逗號停半拍，句號停一拍。
3. 專有名詞慢一點、清楚一點。
4. 讀錯字即改，不要回頭重讀整句。
5. 結尾降調，不要每句都升調。

**練法**：每天朗讀 1 段新聞（約 80 字），錄音，聽回自己的尾音與速度。

## 九、必備句型與詞彙

| 用途 | 句型 |
|---|---|
| 表達意見 | In my opinion, ... / I think ... because ... |

| 讓步 | Although ..., I still ... / ... , but ... |

## 十一、面試當日清單

- 提早 20 分鐘到，先去洗手間。
- 帶：面試通知信、身份證／手冊、鉛筆、水。
- 衣著：整齊校服或素色恤衫長褲，鞋要乾淨。
- 入門敲門、說 Good morning；離開說 Thank you, goodbye.
- 坐直、手放膝上、望住對方；聽不清就說 "Could you repeat the question, please?"
- 完結前準備 1 條問題（見下）。

### 可以反問學校的問題

> - May I ask when we will know the result?
> - Could you tell me more about the robotics club?
> - What kind of students do well in your school?

不要問：功課多不多？有沒有小食部？老師惡不惡？

---

## 十二、家長提示

- **不要寫稿讓孩子背**：背到第三題就露餡。用「關鍵字卡」代替。
- 用「Why?」追問：每次答完問「點解？」迫出例子。
- 錄影不錄音：看到低頭、手勢、眼神。
- 模擬時故意問一條難題，練「不懂時的態度」。
- 平板問題不要完全禁止：把它變成「採訪題材」，例如叫他講解一條科技短片，練英文描述。
- 面試前一日早睡，比臨急多練一小時有用。

---

## 十三、最後提醒

- 你的賣點不是「樣樣第一」，而是 **好奇＋數理＋鋼琴＋肯改**。
- 玩平板不是死穴，**講得出界線與得著**就是加分位。
- 英語有小錯照講，**唔好靜音**。
- 誠實講「我未識，但我會試」，比背金句更得老師歡心。

> 祝面試順利。
